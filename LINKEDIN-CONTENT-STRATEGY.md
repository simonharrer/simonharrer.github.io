You are not "a data influencer." Your edge is the crossover: a PhD and bestselling

that makes enterprise data trustworthy enough for humans **and** AI agents to use. That

rare and defensible. Every post should reinforce one of those three credibility pillars:

1. **Engineer** — I care about correctness, tests, and clean interfaces, applied to data.
2. **Standards steward** — I sit on the ODCS/ODPS Technical Steering Committee at the Linux Foundation; I have skin in the open ecosystem, not just a product to sell.
3. **Founder building in public** — I'm shipping Entropy Data and telling you what I learn.

## 5. Formats & the repurposing engine

You already produce a firehose of raw material — talks, papers, news, open-source releases,
the Data Landscape map. The strategy is **not "create more," it's "atomize what exists."**

**Formats, ranked by current LinkedIn leverage:**
1. **Native text posts** (5–15 lines, one idea, strong first line) — your bread and butter.
2. **Document/carousel PDFs** — diagrams, "anatomy of a data contract," 5-step guides. High save rate, great for the ODCS/data-product explainers.
3. **Personal narrative posts** — founder lessons, "I was wrong about X." Highest trust.
4. **Short visuals / annotated diagrams** — one screenshot from the Editor, CLI output, the Data Landscape map.
5. **Talk / podcast clips** — 30–60s cuts with a written takeaway.
6. **Polls** — occasional, for reach + audience research ("Do you version your data contracts? yes/no/what's a data contract").

**Repurposing pipeline — every big asset becomes ~6 posts:**

```
1 talk / paper / release
        │
        ├─ 1 announcement post (the news)
        ├─ 1 "key takeaway" text post (the single best idea)
        ├─ 1 carousel (the framework / steps)
        ├─ 1 contrarian/opinion post (the spicy angle)
        ├─ 1 short clip or diagram (visual)
        └─ 1 "lessons from doing it" founder post
```

Assets ready to atomize right now: Data Contract CLI v1, the ICWS 2026 data contracts paper,
BARC Spotlight ("A Data Marketplace Is What Your Agents Need"), the MCP server, Semantics / OSI,
Power BI integration, and every 2026 talk (JAX, TDWI, DATA Festival, Data Mesh Belgium).

## 6. Voice & style rules

- **Opinionated, not neutral.** Take a stance ("Data catalogs failed because they described data instead of governing access to it"). Neutrality gets scrolled past.
- **Engineer-to-engineer.** Concrete, specific, example-driven. Show a YAML snippet, a CLI command, a real failure. Avoid consultant-speak and buzzword soup.
- **Generous.** Teach the thing fully; don't tease-and-gate. Credibility compounds.
- **No hype, no AI slop.** Your differentiation is substance. Never post generic "5 AI trends" filler.
- **Structure for the feed:** killer first line (the hook is 80% of the job), whitespace, one idea per post, short paragraphs, end with a question or a light CTA.
- **English-first**, with occasional German posts for the DACH audience (mirrors the /de/ site) — especially for regional events (TDWI München, JAX Mainz) and German-language talks.
- **Soft CTAs only.** ~1 in 5 posts may point to Entropy Data, a talk, or the CLI. The rest earn the right. Prefer "comment X and I'll send it" or a link in the first comment over hard pitches.

Target **4 posts/week** (sustainable > sporadic bursts). A repeatable template:

|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Insight / opinion | 1 Data Contracts or 2 Data Products | Text, strong POV |

| Wed | Teach | 3 Agentic AI × Data | Carousel or diagram |

Post around **08:00–10:00 CET** on weekdays (catches DACH morning + EU + early US). Then:
**spend 30–60 min replying to every comment in the first 2 hours** — early engagement drives reach
more than the post itself. Block it in the calendar; treat it as part of publishing.

## 8. Engagement strategy (the half nobody does)

Posting is 50%. The other 50%:
- **Comment daily** on 5–10 posts from ICP buyers, practitioners, and ecosystem peers (Bitol, OSI, BARC/TDWI analysts, dbt Labs folks, data-mesh voices). Thoughtful comments on bigger accounts are your #1 growth lever.
- **Reply to every comment** on your own posts, fast, and ask a follow-up to extend the thread.
- **DM, don't pitch.** When someone engages repeatedly, open a genuine conversation. Pipeline hides in DMs.
- **Amplify the ecosystem** — reshare Jochen, Entropy Data company posts, Bitol/LF updates with your own added take (never a bare reshare).
- **Co-create** — tag collaborators, quote analysts, feature customers (with permission). Borrowed audiences compound faster than your own.

## 11. Profile optimization (do this before posting more)

- **Headline:** lead with the value + category, not just the title. e.g. _"Co-Founder & CEO, Entropy Data · Making enterprise data trustworthy for humans & AI agents · Data Contracts & Products · ODCS/ODPS @ Linux Foundation"_
- **Featured section:** pin the strongest asset (BARC Spotlight, Data Contract CLI, a flagship talk, entropy-data.com).
- **About:** rewrite in first person around the positioning in §1; one clear CTA (book a demo / try the CLI).
- **Banner:** Entropy Data brand + one-line value prop.
- **Creator mode / topics:** set hashtags to your pillars (#DataContracts #DataProducts #DataMesh #DataEngineering #AgenticAI).
- Consistent link in posts → `entropy-data.com/learn/...` deep pages, which are already SEO-optimized.

## 12. Guardrails

- Don't oversell the product — earn attention with teaching. Max ~1-in-5 posts is a direct CTA.
- Don't chase every trend; only comment on AI/data news where you have a genuine, differentiated take.
- Stay consistent over clever — 4 solid posts/week for a year beats a viral month then silence.
- Protect the brand: no dunking on named competitors, no confidential customer data, no hype you can't back with substance.
